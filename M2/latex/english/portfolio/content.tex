\section{Module Overview}
The Portfolio module provides research-only workflows: discover \texttt{Model Portfolio}, maintain \texttt{My Portfolio}, generate \texttt{AI Portfolio Recommendation}, analyze portfolio drafts, and review saved \texttt{My Portfolio} against historical \texttt{Baseline Snapshot}.
The module is organized by three tabs: \texttt{Recommended} is used to browse administrator-maintained Model Portfolios and start the recommendation quiz; \texttt{My portfolios} is used to create, view, edit, delete, analyze and review user-saved portfolios; \texttt{Watchlist} is used to view and remove Model Portfolios saved for future reference.
\begin{quote}\small Scope: The forecasts, analysis, recommendations and \texttt{Suggested actions} in this module are for research only, do not guarantee returns, and do not trigger brokerage orders or trade execution.\end{quote}

\section{User Story 3 - Generate AI Portfolio Recommendation}
\textbf{Story ID:} \texttt{US-PORT-03}
\textbf{User story:} As a user, I want to answer the fit questionnaire, get interpretable ETF recommendations, check the generated results and save to My portfolios.
\textbf{Video:} \href{https://jjpvro70sief.jp.larksuite.com/wiki/NSZ1wCHloiD8QAkYSDsj9CNtpje}{US-PORT-03 Acceptance Video}
\subsection{Walkthrough}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Open \texttt{Find Your Ideal Portfolio} from \texttt{Portfolio \textgreater{} Recommended}, complete the four-question questionnaire and check the response progress.
\item Submit the answer and observe the generation progress and pending states of the Recommendation.
\item View the resulting risk profile, projection, portfolio metrics, recommendation build, and ETF holdings.
\item Return to the questionnaire to adjust an answer, regenerate and confirm that the results are updated with the new answer.
\item Select \texttt{Add to My Portfolio}, review the configuration, and select \texttt{Add as new} or \texttt{Add to existing}.
