\section{Module Overview}
The Market module provides a complete path from market structure discovery to individual ETF research, comparison and saving. Users first establish market direction in \texttt{Market Intelligence}, then enter \texttt{Sector Detail} to compare ETFs within sectors, then conduct in-depth research in \texttt{ETF Detail}, and save the results to \texttt{Watchlist} or \texttt{My Portfolio}.
\begin{quote}\small Data boundaries: When the page displays delayed quotes, cached snapshots or EOD (end-of-day) data, the corresponding tags must be retained. The Market module is used for research discovery and does not constitute trading advice.\end{quote}

\section{User Story 1 - Establish research direction from \texttt{Market Intelligence}}
\textbf{Story ID:} \texttt{US-MKT-01}
\textbf{User story:} As a user who has not yet selected a specific ETF, I want to understand the day's market breadth, sector movements and major-index context so that I can choose a sector to research next.
\subsection{Walkthrough}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Open \texttt{Market Intelligence}. Show the overall structure of the page first and explain that it is a starting point for ETF research, not a complete ETF directory or trading recommendation page.
\item Browse the \texttt{Sector Heatmap}. Explain that each color block represents a sector or theme basket, showing equal weight changes, ETF volume and small trend lines; it also displays \texttt{Rising}, \texttt{Flat}, and \texttt{Falling} summaries.
\item Browse down to \texttt{AI Market Signals}. Select at least one signal card showing the signal date, market movement, AI analysis summary, related ETFs, and external sources when present.
\item Display \texttt{Market Indices}, including Hang Seng Index, Hang Seng Tech and S\&P 500 Index, and keep the delayed data label visible.
\item Browse \texttt{Market Discovery Rail}. In the \texttt{Sectors} scope, show \texttt{Default} and \texttt{Hot} and explain that they provide stable entry points and sectors with large moves that day, respectively.
\item Show the symbol, name, price, daily move and \texttt{View all} action in \texttt{Recommended ETFs}.
\item Click on a section (it is recommended to use \texttt{Crypto}), enter \texttt{Sector Detail}, complete the current story and connect to the next story.
\end{enumerate}

\section{User Story 2 - Understand sector trends and filter ETFs}
\textbf{Story ID:} \texttt{US-MKT-02}
\textbf{User story:} As a user who enters a certain sector from the market homepage, I hope to understand the breadth, liquidity and risk background of changes in the sector, compare ETFs within the sector, and select the next research target.
\subsection{Walkthrough}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Open a sector from \texttt{Sector Heatmap} or \texttt{Market Discovery Rail}. Use a sector with complete data, such as \texttt{Crypto}.
\item Display the top sector banner: sector name, representative tickers, number of ETFs in the basket, latest sector changes, and cached sector snapshot label.
\item Browse \texttt{AI Sector Insights}. \texttt{Momentum \& Breadth}, \texttt{Liquidity Pulse}, \texttt{Trend \& Risk} are displayed in order, indicating that these contents are based on EOD cached ETF data and are not real-time predictions.
\item Go to \texttt{ETF Rankings}. Confirm that the list only contains ETFs within the selected sector.
\item Switch 1 to 2 sorting indicators in sequence, such as \texttt{Change}, \texttt{Volume} or \texttt{Expense Ratio}; switch the return period again, such as \texttt{1D} to \texttt{1M}.
\item Select an ETF and enter \texttt{ETF Detail} to complete the transition from sector discovery to product research.
\end{enumerate}

\section{User Story 3 - Research and compare individual ETFs}
\textbf{Story ID:} \texttt{US-MKT-03}
\textbf{User story:} As a user who has selected a candidate ETF, I would like to view quotes, candlesticks, key statistics and news, and compare relative movements to another ETF.
\subsection{Walkthrough}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Open an ETF with complete data from \texttt{ETF Rankings}, \texttt{Recommended ETFs}, \texttt{All}, \texttt{Watchlist} or ETF movers.
\item Display detailed headers: fund name, symbol, exchange, latest price, currency, daily change and market/\allowbreak{}close status.
\item Switch at least two time ranges among \texttt{1D}, \texttt{1W}, \texttt{1M} and \texttt{3M} on the K-line chart.
\item Scrub the chart to display open, high, low, close and volume updates at the selected time point.
\item Open the \texttt{View} panel, switch the chart type once, and turn on or off at least one moving average (\texttt{MA5}, \texttt{MA10} or \texttt{MA30}).
\item Switch between \texttt{Overview} and \texttt{News}. Check key stats in \texttt{Overview}; display headline, source and timestamp in \texttt{News}.
\item Open \texttt{More}, go to \texttt{Compare ETF}, select the second ETF and view the normalized overlay under the same chart option.
\end{enumerate}

\section{User Story 4 - Save ETF Research Results}
\textbf{Story ID:} \texttt{US-MKT-04}
\textbf{User story:} As a user who has completed preliminary research, I want to save an ETF to \texttt{Watchlist} for continued tracking, add it to an existing \texttt{My Portfolio} for further portfolio analysis, or continue screening through the unified \texttt{Screener}.
\subsection{Walkthrough}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Click the star icon in \texttt{ETF Detail} to add the ETF to \texttt{Watchlist}.
\item Open the \texttt{Watchlist} tab of \texttt{Screener} and confirm that the ETF appears; then remove it and confirm that the list is updated simultaneously.
\item Return to \texttt{ETF Detail}, click the plus button to open the \texttt{Add to existing portfolio} sheet.
\item Display selected ETF, latest price, lots and estimated market value. Note: 1 lot is calculated as 100 shares.
\item Display the duplicate holding status: the portfolio that already contains the ETF displays a duplicate prompt and is unselectable; then select another eligible portfolio to save.
\item Re-open the target \texttt{My Portfolio} and confirm that the ETF has been saved as a holding record. It is emphasized that this process does not place orders or execute transactions.
\item Return to \texttt{Screener}, display the three data ranges of \texttt{Recommended}, \texttt{All}, and \texttt{Watchlist} in sequence, and operate search, filter and sort once.
\end{enumerate}
